# Tōge Round Seven

Vertical, phone-first campaign page for November 20–22, 2026 in Eureka Springs, Arkansas. Based on the existing Reaction Motorsports app’s photography, logos, black background, and Tōge green accents.

## Standalone GitHub campaign

Publish the contents of this folder at the root of a separate repository named `toge-round-seven`. Enable GitHub Pages from the `main` branch, root folder. No build or installation is required.

This is a standalone marketing and feedback site. Do not add it to the live events app repository or the main website navigation. The footer logo links directly to https://reactionmotorsportsevents.com.

Open `index.html` for a local preview. For native sharing and clipboard features, serve over localhost or HTTPS. The share button uses the visitor’s native share sheet when available, then a clipboard/manual-copy fallback. A local preview shares event details without a localhost link; the hosted page shares its own URL.

## Content

- Friday, November 20: 6 PM town cruise followed by the free car show, live DJ, and food trucks.
- Saturday and Sunday, November 21–22: gates and drifting at 9 AM; drifting ends at 5 PM both days.
- $10 per person per day on Saturday and Sunday. Kids 12 and under free. Cash and cards at the gate. No online ticket sales.
- Walking trails, viewing areas, shuttle buses every 45 minutes, chairs and outdoor footwear, restrooms, parking volunteers, and rain-or-shine guidance.
- Great Passion Play, 935 Passion Play Road, Eureka Springs, AR 72632. Map link from the event app’s venue address.

Friday cruise leaves Great Passion Play at 6 PM for Gaskin Switch, 2039 E Van Buren Ste A, Eureka Springs, AR 72632. Arrival around 6:10 PM; free car show with DJ and food trucks until 8:30 PM, then return cruise to Great Passion Play. Saturday church is at 6 PM under the Christ of the Ozarks statue. Both sections use user-provided photos. No claim is made that parking or shuttles are free. No driver registration or driver pricing appears.
